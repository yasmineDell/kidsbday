Kids' Birthdays
at WOW Museum

Book button

Looking for a unique, stress-free idea for the next birthday party? At WOW Museum Munich, your child's special day will be an unforgettable experience filled with fun, wonder, and creativity.

What to expect

Children are immersed in a fascinating world of illusions where they can experiment, marvel, and laugh. Our interactive exhibits offer exciting discoveries and countless photo opportunities. The WOW Museum provides long-lasting fun and creates unforgettable memories.

WOW B-Day Tour

During the WOW B-Day Tour, the group get a glimpse behind the scenes of the illusions. Our guides introduce the children to the WOW Museum in a playful way and reveal lots of tips and tricks. The puzzles in the museum are solved and the hiding places of Willow, the museum gnome, are discovered as a team.
